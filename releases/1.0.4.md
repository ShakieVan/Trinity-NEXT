# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.4 — a mirrored field, hidden stones, figures and badges

- **A new shape of field:** nine, eleven or thirteen stones in a row instead of ten, twelve or fourteen, and always an even number of rows. The jags of the left and the right edge now mirror each other.
- **One hidden stone for every row:** at each end of a row half a triangle is missing to the edge of the screen. The two halves are one stone that lies outside the field; its colour shows in both jags as if through frosted glass. It does not count until it comes in. You can pull it out in any direction, across or along a slant, and when stones slide it is the first to come in. With it every line has an even number of places, so no stone comes back in turned over any more (in 1.0.3 a short slanted line at a side edge did that).
- **Figures:** some shapes are worth more than their stones. *Delta*: four stones of one colour forming a large triangle leave a glider in their middle; gliders now come from deltas only, no longer from the edge. *Comb*: six stones around one point take the eighteen stones around them along. *Chain*: a group of eight or more counts double, of twelve or more threefold. *Colour storm*: a bomb cleared together with a prism takes every stone of its colour off the field.
- **Clamped stones slide along** when the phone is tilted. Their three lines still cannot be dragged.
- **Badges:** 22 of them, for figures, streaks, scores and more. A button on the title screen lists them.
- **A game in progress goes on** on the new field: without its last column, and without its last row if their number was odd. What lay there is gone. The lists of best games stay; the entries in them so far were made on the slightly larger fields.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); the updates to 1.0.2 and 1.0.3 on the same emulator were not blocked. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. At the update to this version, on that emulator on November 25, 2026, Play Protect recommended a scan because it did not know the app; the scan was not requested, and the second start of the installation went through. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **More single gaps at the side edges:** a single gap right beside a hidden stone stays, like any single gap, because that stone points the other way. In test runs about 13 to 16 percent of the places stood empty, against 9 to 11 percent on the fields of 1.0.3.
- **The new field, the frosted glass and the figures were seen on an Android 16 emulator only,** not yet on a phone held in the hand. There the signed version was played by hand for a few moves, tilted both ways.
- **A crystal shrinking and its stone vanishing, and most of the badges, were checked by the app's automatic tests or not at all,** not seen on a screen.
- **Hardly played by hand on a real phone yet.** Tilting on real hardware, the sound from a phone's speaker and the vibration have not been checked systematically.
- **The sounds were chosen by measurement, not by ear.**
- The difficulty of the Challenge mode is a first setting and may change.
