# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.3 — gliders with a crystal, the wait is yours to set

- **Gliders carry a crystal:** a glider is now a grey stone without a colour, and it never matches. A line it lies on can be moved without a match; the crystal on it shows how often. It comes with one to five charges, gets smaller with each one used, and with the last one the stone is gone and leaves a gap. Moves that match cost nothing. (The double arrow the glider used to show told nobody what it did.)
- **The wait before sliding is an option:** "Nachrutschen" in the options offers "Schnell" (fast, 0.25 s), "Mittel" (medium, 0.5 s), "Langsam" (slow, 0.75 s) and "Gemütlich" (cosy, 1 s, the setting of 1.0.2 and still the one a new installation starts with).
- **No countdown:** the ring that showed the wait running out is gone again.
- A game saved by an earlier version goes on; a glider in it comes back with one charge.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); the update to 1.0.2 on the same emulator was not blocked. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. The update to this version, again on that emulator, was installed at the first attempt without any Play Protect dialog. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **A crystal shrinking and its stone vanishing were checked by the app's automatic tests only,** not yet seen on a screen: the self-playing test run always picks moves that match. The glider with its crystal was seen in a game and in the instructions on an Android 16 emulator.
- **Hardly played by hand on a real phone yet.** Tilting on real hardware, the sound from a phone's speaker and the vibration have not been checked systematically.
- **The sounds were chosen by measurement, not by ear.**
- The difficulty of the Challenge mode is a first setting and may change.
