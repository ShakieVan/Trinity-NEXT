# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.9 — the stone you hold glows

- **The stone you drag a line by now glows:** a bright, gently pulsing light around its edges, and the stone itself a little lighter. Together with the white outline that marks where it lay (since 1.0.5), you see at a glance which stone you hold and where it has to go back to if you would rather not make the move. The glow follows the stone round the end of the line.

Nothing else has changed since 1.0.8.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the updates to versions 1.0.7 and 1.0.8 in tests on November 25, 2026,** and the update to 1.0.1 the day before, all on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested). VirusTotal found nothing in 1.0.8 (0 of 65 scanners, Google's among them), and an appeal for 1.0.8 was filed with Google the same day; Google states that it does not reply. This version, 1.0.9, has not been submitted to either. At the update to it, on that emulator a little later the same day, Play Protect only recommended a scan because it did not know the app; the scan was not requested, and the second start of the installation went through. Why this version was not blocked is not known. The classification is unexplained and not cleared, and nothing here is a clearance. Read Play Protect's message on your device and decide for yourself. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **The glow was seen on an Android 16 emulator,** in the signed version, not yet on a phone held in the hand.
- The known limits of 1.0.8 still apply. [Notes of 1.0.8](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.8.md)
