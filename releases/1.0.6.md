# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.6 — four ways to tilt, each explained in a moving picture

- **Two new ways of measuring the tilt** join the two of 1.0.5, which now go by the names *Gyroscope* (was "Own position") and *Gravity*:
  - **Car ride:** you set your position as with Gyroscope, but the tilt is measured against gravity alone. A bend in the road or a swivel chair, which Gyroscope takes for tilting, changes nothing here, and the position does not wander. To tilt sideways, lower the left or the right edge; with the phone held steeply that is a turn like that of a steering wheel. The top edge is tipped away from you as before.
  - **Acceleration:** here it is not how the phone is held that counts, but how fast you tip it. A short jerk, an edge quickly away from you, makes the stones slide towards it; bringing the phone back slowly does nothing. Once the field has come to rest, nothing slides again until the next jerk. There is nothing to set.
- **A "?" beside "Tilt sensing" in the options** ("Lageerkennung" in German) opens a sheet that explains all four ways, each with a moving picture of a phone being tipped and a few sentences.
- The options show the four ways two to a row. Gyroscope stays what everyone starts with; a choice made in 1.0.5 is kept.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Neither of the new ways has been tried with a phone held in the hand, and Car ride not in a car.** On an Android 16 emulator the signed version was set upright in Car ride; lowering the left edge made the sign point left, tipping the top edge away made it point up. A jerk imitated with the emulator's gyroscope gave its direction, and the direction ended by itself. That a bend changes nothing in Car ride, and that neither a hand swinging back nor a bump nor a slow bend counts as a jerk, is covered by the app's automatic tests only.
- **In a bend driven fast** the stones are pushed outwards in Car ride all the same, like a marble on a tray. How much Android's gravity sensor softens that has not been measured.
- **Car ride with the screen facing straight down** (lying on your back, the phone overhead): which edge counts as left and which as right is not well defined there. Use Gyroscope for that.
- **What makes a jerk is a first setting** (faster than 40 degrees a second, 5.5 degrees in one go) and may change.
- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); later updates on the same emulator were not blocked. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- The known limits of 1.0.5 still apply, among them: tilting from a chosen position has hardly been tried by hand, and with Gyroscope the chosen position may wander when the phone is held upright. [Notes of 1.0.5](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.5.md)
