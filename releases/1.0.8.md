# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.8 — the way back is no jerk, and a slider for how hard

Three more changes to *Acceleration*, the way of tilting everyone starts with, after the owner had tried 1.0.7 on a phone:

- **Bringing the phone back is no jerk any more.** In 1.0.7 simply moving the phone back to where it was gave the opposite direction far too easily. The app now keeps track of how far the phone is from where it rested; the way back there never counts, however fast.
- **The opposite direction takes more.** If you got it wrong, jerk clearly harder the other way (two and a half times as fast as a jerk has to be), or tip the phone on past where it rested. An ordinary jerk the other way counts again once the phone is back where it was or has been held still for a moment.
- **A slider for the sensitivity.** Under **Options › Tilt sensing**, with Acceleration chosen, a slider sets how hard a jerk has to be, more sensitive to the right. A sign beside it shows every jerk the app recognises, so you can try the setting right there. Its middle, where everyone starts, is less sensitive than 1.0.7 was; what 1.0.7 took for a jerk lies at about four fifths of the way to the right.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.7 in a test on November 25, 2026,** as it had blocked the update to 1.0.1 the day before, both on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested). The classification is unexplained and not cleared, and nothing here is a clearance. Read Play Protect's message on your device and decide for yourself. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **The changed behaviour was checked on an Android 16 emulator,** with jerks imitated through its gyroscope: the slider and its sign, a turn that is a jerk at the sensitive end and none at the other, a jerk followed by an ordinary move back (no opposite direction), a jerk followed by a much harder one back (the opposite at once). With a phone held in the hand the owner tried Acceleration as it was in 1.0.7, not yet as it is now.
- **In a car going round a tight bend** the app may take the phone to stand off from where it rested; for a moment an ordinary jerk against the bend then counts only if it is a hard one. Held still, that passes within a second or two.
- The known limits of 1.0.6 still apply, among them: Car ride has not been tried in a car. [Notes of 1.0.6](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.6.md)
