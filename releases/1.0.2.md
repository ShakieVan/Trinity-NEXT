# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.2 — a second to tilt

- **Sliding waits a second:** after stones have vanished, the rest stay put for one second and then slide the way the phone is tilted at that moment. That leaves time to tilt it another way, also between the steps of a streak. A ring running out in the middle of the field shows the wait, with the direction inside.
- The wait is skipped when nothing would slide anyway: with the phone lying flat, or when no stone has room to move. Tilting then starts the sliding at once, as before.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested). On the owner's Galaxy S24 that update was installed. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. The update to 1.0.2 on the same emulator was not blocked: Play Protect only recommended a scan, which was not requested, and the installation went through on the second start. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **The new wait was only seen on an Android 16 emulator** before publication; whether one second feels right with a phone in the hand is still to be found out.
- **Hardly played by hand on a real phone yet.** Tilting on real hardware, the sound from a phone's speaker and the vibration have not been checked systematically.
- **The sounds were chosen by measurement, not by ear.**
- The difficulty of the Challenge mode is a first setting and may change.
