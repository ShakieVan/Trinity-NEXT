# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.0 — first public version

A tilt-and-slide puzzle. The field is made of triangular stones in five colours. Drag a stone and its whole line glides along; what leaves the field at one end comes back in at the other. Three or more stones of one colour vanish. Then tilt the phone: the rest slide into the gaps the way you tilt it.

- **Move a line** across or along either slant. It rests a whole number of triangle widths further and springs back if nothing matches.
- **Tilt to slide** in one of six directions. Stones slide without turning over, so a single gap stays until something next to it vanishes. Lying flat, nothing slides.
- **Streaks:** clears that follow from sliding count ×2, ×3, ×4 … The sound climbs with them; every fifth step sets off fireworks.
- **Special stones:** prism (every colour), glider (one move without a match), clamp (a stone and its three lines are held), bomb (counts down with every move).
- **Two modes:** "Herausforderung" (Challenge, with clamps and bombs) and "Entspannt" (Relaxed, neither and no end).
- **Three looks** ("Edelsteine", "Neon", "Flach") and **three field sizes**, each with its own list of best games.
- **Saved after every move.** German and English.
- **Updates from this page:** "Updates" on the title screen checks, downloads and installs a newer version when you ask it to.
- No advertising, no purchases, no account.

## How to install

Download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Not yet played by hand on a real phone.** It was played on an Android 16 emulator. On a Galaxy S21 (Android 15) this file was installed over a test build and started without an error, and the test build played itself smoothly there with the phone lying flat. Tilting on real hardware, the sound from a phone's speaker and the vibration were not checked before publication.
- **The sounds were chosen by measurement, not by ear.** They were made with a text-to-sound model; which of the candidates sounds best is still to be decided by listening.
- **Google Play Protect does not know this app** and may recommend a scan when you install it. No scan was requested for this version, so Play Protect has not rated it. See [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- The difficulty of the Challenge mode (how soon clamps and bombs come, how long a bomb gives you) is a first setting and may change.
