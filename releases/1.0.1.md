# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.1 — a better app icon

- **App icon:** it now shows the three stones of the title screen, all the same size, evenly spaced and with rounded tips. The first icon was drawn by hand and its triangles differed in size and sat slightly off.
- A plain version of the same shapes is used where Android shows single-colour icons.

Nothing in the game itself has changed since 1.0.0.

## How to install

If you have 1.0.0: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

The limits of 1.0.0 still apply:

- **Not yet played by hand on a real phone.** Tilting on real hardware, the sound from a phone's speaker and the vibration are unverified. This version was installed and played on an Android 16 emulator only.
- **The sounds were chosen by measurement, not by ear.**
- **Google Play Protect does not know this app** and may recommend a scan when you install it. No scan was requested for this version, so Play Protect has not rated it. See [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- The difficulty of the Challenge mode is a first setting and may change.
