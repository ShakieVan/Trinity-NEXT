# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.7 — nudge every movement with a jerk

After the owner had tried *Acceleration* on a phone, three things changed about it:

- **It is what everyone starts with now.** Whoever has not chosen a way of measuring the tilt in the options plays with Acceleration from this version on, existing installations too. A choice made in the options is kept. Nothing has to be set before a game in this way; the other three (Gravity, Gyroscope, Car ride) are still there under **Options › Tilt sensing**.
- **One jerk, one movement.** A quick jerk nudges the stones: they slide that way once, and at once nothing slides again. Whatever could slide next, in a streak too, waits for another jerk; the streak goes on counting. In 1.0.6 a jerk held until the whole field had come to rest. A jerk that sets nothing off now stays good only for the wait before sliding set in the options plus a fifth of a second, instead of a second and a half.
- **Nothing holds a second jerk back.** In 1.0.6 a quick move the other way within 0.8 seconds was taken for the hand swinging back and passed over. That is gone: if you got it wrong, the next jerk counts at once, even without the hand coming to rest in between.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **A hand that swings back fast now gives the opposite direction.** That is the price of the second point above; bring the phone back slowly.
- **The changed behaviour was checked on an Android 16 emulator,** with jerks imitated through its gyroscope: a fresh start without setting a position, one jerk for one slide, the gaps of the match that followed staying until the next jerk, a jerk with nothing to slide ending by itself. With a phone held in the hand the owner tried Acceleration as it was in 1.0.6, not yet as it is now.
- **What makes a jerk is still a first setting** (faster than 40 degrees a second, 5.5 degrees in one go) and may change.
- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); later updates on the same emulator were not blocked. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- The known limits of 1.0.6 still apply, among them: Car ride has not been tried in a car. [Notes of 1.0.6](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.6.md)
