# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.5 — play in any position, a marker for the way back, thriftier gliders

- **Tilt from a position of your own:** until now the stones slid the way the earth pulled, so the phone had to be held about level. Now, before a game begins or goes on, you hold the phone still for five seconds the way you want to play: flat, upright, on its side, lying down. A dot has to stay inside a circle that long; if it leaves, the count starts over from the position the phone is in at that moment. That position then counts as lying flat: tip an edge away from you and the stones slide towards it. Coming back from the pause sets the position anew, and so does a tap on the sign at the top right. This is what everyone starts with now, existing installations too. **Options › Tilt sensing** ("Lageerkennung" in German) switches back to gravity, where there is nothing to set.
- **A small black arrow in the sign at the top right** shows how the phone is tilted in fact: which way exactly and how far, not snapped to one of the six directions.
- **A marker for the way back:** while you drag a line, a white outline shows where your stone lay. Bring it back there before you let go and no move is made.
- **A glider pays only for a move made with it:** a line stays where you put it without a match only if you dragged it by the glider itself. Dragged by another of its stones, the line springs back as usual and the crystal is left untouched. Up to 1.0.4 any glider on the line paid.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Tilting from a chosen position has not been tried with a phone held in the hand.** A test build on a Galaxy S21 lying still showed the calibration, the count and the start of the game. On an Android 16 emulator the signed version was calibrated with the virtual phone upright; tipping its top edge away made the sign point up the screen. Tilting sideways from an upright position could not be imitated there and is covered by the app's automatic tests only.
- **The chosen position may wander.** The sensor used follows the phone's turning with the gyroscope and steadies it with gravity, without the compass; it cannot hold the turning around the vertical for ever. With the phone held upright, the position that counts as flat may therefore drift slowly to one side. How fast has not been measured. A tap on the sign sets it anew.
- **A phone without that sensor** stays with gravity; the option is not offered there.
- **The new rule for gliders was checked by the app's automatic tests,** not by finger on a screen.
- **Google Play Protect blocked the update to version 1.0.1 in a test on November 24, 2026** on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); later updates on the same emulator were not blocked. The classification is unexplained and not cleared. At the update to this version, on that emulator on November 25, 2026, Play Protect recommended a scan because it did not know the app; the scan was not requested, and the second start of the installation went through. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- The known limits of 1.0.4 still apply: more single gaps at the side edges, hardly played by hand on a real phone, sounds chosen by measurement, a first setting of the Challenge mode. [Notes of 1.0.4](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.4.md)
