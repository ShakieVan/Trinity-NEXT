# Trinity-NEXT 1.0.10 — the crystal star and the crystal rush

Six gliders lying around one point used to be nothing, because a glider has no colour and never matches. The owner found such hexagons in two of his games and wanted them to do something wild. Now they do:

- **Crystal star:** when a glider lies in each of the six places around one point, their crystals burst, by themselves like any match, also when sliding brings the six together. Rays in all six directions take every stone on the six lines through that point along: bombs, clamped stones, prisms and further gliders too; the hidden stones beside the field stay. On the small field that is 66 of its 90 stones. The larger the six crystals, the more points: 100 for every charge they hold and 20 for every stone on the rays. A move that completes the star holds like a match and costs no crystal.
- **Crystal rush:** for the next six moves the streak does not start over with your move but keeps climbing with every clear. The bar above the field counts the moves that are left ("Rush ×3 · 4 left"), and the frame of the field glows pale blue as long as the rush lasts. It is saved with the game.
- **A badge** for the crystal star; there are 23 badges now.
- **A game in progress** in which six gliders already lie around one point sets the star off as soon as you go on with it.

## How to install

If you have an earlier version: open **Updates** on the title screen. Otherwise download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- **Google Play Protect blocked the updates to versions 1.0.7 and 1.0.8 in tests on November 25, 2026,** and the update to 1.0.1 the day before, all on an Android 16 emulator ("Diese App könnte schädlich sein", this app may be harmful, without naming a reason and without a scan having been requested); the update to 1.0.9 was not blocked there. VirusTotal found nothing in 1.0.8 (0 of 65 scanners, Google's among them), and an appeal for 1.0.8 was filed with Google the same day; Google states that it does not reply. This version, 1.0.10, has not been submitted to either. The update to it through the app has not been tried yet as these notes are written; what Play Protect shows then is recorded in [INSTALLATION.md](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#update-results). The classification is unexplained and not cleared, and nothing here is a clearance. Read Play Protect's message on your device and decide for yourself. Details: [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
- **The star and the rush were seen on an Android 16 emulator only,** not yet on a phone held in the hand: the star going off in a saved game in the signed version, and the rush counting down and ending in a test build that played itself.
- **The streak badges come easier in a rush,** because ×5 and ×10 are reached quickly there.
- **The star has no sound of its own yet.** It rings with the sound of a glider, the crackle of the fireworks and the fanfare of the figures.
- The points of the star and the length of the rush are a first setting and may change.
- The known limits of 1.0.9 still apply. [Notes of 1.0.9](https://github.com/ShakieVan/Trinity-NEXT/blob/main/releases/1.0.9.md)
